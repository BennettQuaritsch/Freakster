# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Music and board game enthusiasts who use Freakster to generate physical song cards from Spotify playlists. They play music guessing games with friends, at parties, or game nights. The context is social, fun, low-stakes; the interface should feel like unwrapping a game, not operating software.

## Product Purpose

Freakster converts Spotify playlists into printable, foldable song cards. Each card has a QR code on the front and song details on the back. The cards are used for music trivia, guessing games, and social play. Success: a user goes from playlist URL to printed cards in under two minutes, with zero friction.

## Brand Personality

Bold, playful, nocturnal. Late-night game energy. A record store that stays open past midnight, crossed with the tactile joy of unboxing a new board game. Confident without being loud; fun without being childish.

## Anti-references

- Corporate SaaS: no enterprise dashboards, no blue-gray blandness, no metric cards, no "workspace" language.
- Spotify imitation: no green-black palette, no streaming-app layout patterns. Freakster is its own thing.
- Generic "modern web app": no identical card grids, no hero sections with stock photography, no gradient buttons.

## Design Principles

1. **Game first, tool second.** Every interaction should feel like part of the game, not like operating software. The UI is a game board, not a control panel.
2. **One path, no detours.** The flow is linear: connect, paste, review, print. Remove anything that doesn't serve that path.
3. **Tactile anticipation.** The digital experience should build excitement for the physical cards. Make the screen feel like it's preparing something real.
4. **Nocturnal confidence.** Dark, warm, sure of itself. No hedging with safe grays. Commit to the palette.
5. **Instant legibility.** Despite the bold aesthetic, every label, status, and action must be immediately clear. Playful doesn't mean confusing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Standard contrast ratios, keyboard navigation, focus indicators. Ensure color-coded statuses always have a non-color fallback (icon or text label).
